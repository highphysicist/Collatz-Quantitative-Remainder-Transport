# AI disclosure

OpenAI language models accessed through ChatGPT and Codex contributed
substantially to:

- exploration and formulation of candidate synthesis theorems;
- generation and repair of Lean proofs;
- compatibility debugging across Lean versions;
- proof-audit and build scripting;
- manuscript and repository drafting.

The human project author selected the research direction, supplied and
assembled upstream materials, ran the formal builds, evaluated intermediate
claims, directed the theorem sequence, and assumes responsibility for the
released statements and artifacts.

AI systems are not listed as mathematical authors or independent referees.
Compilation by Lean establishes that the formal declarations follow from their
formal dependencies and reported axioms; it does not independently establish
novelty, exposition quality, or correctness of informal interpretations.

The upstream Mazur formalization and the Lau/AxiomProver refinement have their
own AI disclosures, which remain separate from this project's disclosure.
